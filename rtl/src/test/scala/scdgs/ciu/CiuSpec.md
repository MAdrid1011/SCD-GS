# CIU Tests

`CiuSpec` keeps the prior training summary fixed and pairs an identical
low-support residency record with user and training current views. The user
result is filter, while training is conservatively light.
A structural reset independently forces full work. A second vector uses a
non-diagonal covariance and non-unit `JCov` to check projected support, color,
and every `P/kappa/rho/omega` decision field. It also proves that CIU exposes
the support proxy separately, preserves the retained SIU summary, and carries
the current-view `area` and `radius` without rewriting them.

A saturation-boundary vector drives the activation, support, and all three
low-order color terms to one, and checks that `P` remains exactly one.

An incomplete four-sample history is also applied to both an active and a
candidate record. The active record must take the full path; the candidate
exception is allowed to take the filter path.

A mismatched current-view frame or incomplete StateSPM response is consumed,
counted, and cannot issue work.
